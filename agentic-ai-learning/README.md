# Agentic AI from Scratch

Learning materials for a path from programming and machine-learning foundations through designing, evaluating, and deploying agentic AI. The folders hold the research, study notes, slide decks, a no-code course, QA reviews, and unposted Instagram drafts.

## Google Slides

- **Main:** https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1aJUS8vI3A-thaAhUby-DJnEqovnSaxuDbiahxIB4YFc/edit?usp=drivesdk
- **Beginner:** https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/18XrKk4F5aGJI6A3Y3Y4Bd2LknEX8lrjnVbBqv-upGjg/edit?usp=drivesdk
- **No-code:** https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1UtRiWNxB7PZZ9y5OPh16a7e-NgkGTGH_wKFFZmiPKv0/edit?usp=drivesdk

## notes/

- `notes/agentic-ai-notes.md` — Main learning-path notes. College-grade study notes for optional Stage 0 plus Stages 1–10, from programming and ML foundations through deploying agentic AI (compiled from the research and addendum, 16 Oct 2026).
- `notes/agentic-ai-notes-beginner.md` — Beginner learning-path notes. A plain-language companion for readers who have never written code, covering the same Stage 0–10 path.

## research/

- `research/agentic-ai-learning-path.md` — Research learning path. A structured beginner-to-mastery path (compiled 16 Oct 2026) with concepts, time estimates, and checked resources for each stage.
- `research/agentic-ai-research-addendum.md` — Research addendum. Source-checked responses to gaps in the learning path (checked 16 Oct 2026), including corrections, a prerequisite check, and a beginner glossary.

## decks/

Local PowerPoint copies of the three Google Slides decks above.

- `decks/agentic-ai-deck.pptx` — Main slide deck (20 slides): the 10-stage learning path plus an optional Stage 0 on-ramp.
- `decks/agentic-ai-deck-beginner.pptx` — Beginner slide deck (23 slides): the same path written for people who have never written code.
- `decks/no-code-agentic-ai-deck.pptx` — No-code slide deck (19 slides): six modules and 20 lessons for building agents in a browser.

## course/

- `course/no-code-agentic-ai-course.md` — No-code crash course for people who have never coded. Six modules, from what AI, LLMs, and agents are through prompting, automation, building agents, and keeping them safe and tested.

## qa/

- `qa/review-01.md` — First QA review of the research, notes, course, three decks, and Instagram drafts. Five items failed and were sent back for fixes.
- `qa/review-02.md` — Second QA review. Re-review of the items that failed review 01; those items passed. The Instagram drafts stayed unposted.
